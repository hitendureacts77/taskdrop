### L4-2 · Suspend and dispute decision panels do not take focus, are not dialogs, and Escape does not close them
- Level: Low · Area: person page "Suspend", job page "Refund ..." / "Pay ..." · Class: UI · Where: admin
- Steps: open a person page, press Suspend; read `document.activeElement` (BODY), look for `role=dialog` (none); press Escape (panel stays). Same on the dispute decision panel ("Decide for Asha (seed poster)?").
- Current: an inline panel appears above the page, focus stays on the page body, a keyboard user has to tab through the whole page to reach "Yes, suspend" or Cancel, screen readers are not told a confirmation opened, Escape does nothing.
- Expected: the panel is announced (role alertdialog or live region), focus goes to the reason box or the safe button (Cancel), Escape cancels.
- Proof: JS check on the live page (`activeElement` BODY, no `[role=dialog]`, panel still present after Escape).

### L4-3 · A dispute decision (final, moves frozen money) can be saved with no reason, and the page shows only the poster's side
- Level: Medium · Area: Disputes, job page "Decide this dispute" · Class: Both · Where: admin
- Steps: open a disputed job (`/tasks/7e9239c5-...`), press "Refund Asha (seed poster)": the panel has "Reason (saved with the decision)" with `required=false`, and the "Yes, refund" button is enabled with the box empty. The page says "Both choices are final."
- Current: (1) the reason is optional in the panel and in the database function (`admin_resolve_dispute(p_task_id, p_outcome, p_note default null)`, `supabase/migrations/20261018130000_100_staff_role_db_guards.sql:71`), so an irreversible money decision can leave no written reason. (2) "Why it was reported" shows only the poster's sentence ("End-to-end test: the work was not what was agreed."); the page text has no worker reply, no proof-of-work files, no chat, no earlier decisions, although the panel says "Read the job, the quotes and the reviews first". (3) Only two outcomes exist (pay worker, refund poster), no split.
- Expected: a required reason (at least a few words) on both client and server; the evidence the decider needs (worker reply, proof files, conversation) on the same page.
- Proof: page text of the disputed job, `required:false`, `disabled:false` on the confirm button; migration line above; admin repo `src/components/views/TaskView.tsx:130` and `:152` (`field={{ name: 'note', label: 'Reason (saved with the decision)', ... }}` with no `required`), while the other irreversible panels set `required: true` (`AdminConfigViews.tsx:132` End all staff sessions, `PayoutDetailView.tsx:237`, `CopyrightView.tsx:201`, `ModerationViews.tsx:71,101,188`). (2) and (3) may be product choices; the owner should decide.
