TaskDrop Map D33

Snap-style map: the phone map gets the same engine as the web map, so it scrolls smoothly with thousands of pins.

Try it

  • Switch Earn and Hire at the top. The My people chip sits beside it.
  • Press + and - to move between city and street zoom. Zooming in spreads each group on a ring; zooming out folds it back. Zoomed out, Earn groups show the area's best price.
  • Tap a group: the map flies in. Tap a single pin: a soft "Approximate area" circle appears and the pull-up bar rises with that item.
  • Drag the map to pan. Blue "Just posted" pins ripple every 3.6 s.

Colour rules, unchanged

Earn is always royal blue and shows tasks only. Hire is always jade and shows helpers only.

Earn: tasksHire: helpers

What changes, what stays

Only the pins, clusters and motion change. Location sheet, filters, reach, pull-up bar, You marker and My people layer stay as they are today. The full list is further down.

New in D33 (the actual upgrade)

  • Native engine on phone (same as web): thousands of pins at smooth 60 fps, no React view per pin.
  • Same map look on web and phone; no Google Maps key in the Android app.
  • Animated cluster burst and fold, smoother fly-to, soft pulse on the active dot.
  • 3D buildings at street zoom (16+), if you say yes.
