1.19 F, G, I: a simpler Home and tabs

Design only, for your OK. Every feature stays; nothing is removed. Home answers three things in order: what is happening near me, what do I need done, what needs me now. Everything else is one tap away, in a sheet or a chip, or lives in the tab it belongs to. My tasks cards show status, the one next step, the price, the person and the date at a glance.

Style words: warm and trustworthy, calm and clear, local and quick. Hire is always jade, Earn always royal blue (guests too). Gold = waiting on you, coral = a problem, violet only for the AI writer. Copy is taken from the app's source; names, areas and prices are sample data.

1. The walk: what a new customer and a new worker meet today

Walked on taskdrop.in as a guest in Hire and Earn at 390 x 844 (25 Nov), and read every tab's source for the signed-in parts (My tasks, My work, Wallet, Profile). Hire Home is about 5.8 phone screens tall and Earn Home about 5.5 for a guest, before any tasks of your own are added.

2a. Hire Home, simpler

Eight short blocks instead of fourteen. The four idea sections become one Ideas block with chips; "Why hiring here is safer" becomes one line in the hero that opens the same three points; "Busy on TaskDrop" joins Just posted in the live sheet you open from "active now"; the feature-vote card goes to Profile.

New customer, signed in, two tasks live. First screen: area and live count, Just posted, the ask box, what needs you. Map, Only on TaskDrop, Ideas and Services follow.

Tap "12 active now": the live sheet. Just posted (full list) and Busy on TaskDrop now live here, one tap from Home, still on Explore's Busy now tab.

2b. Earn Home, simpler

The level card and the search become one hero. Matches your skills, Jobs near you, Remote & online and Needed today showed the same jobs again and again; they become one "Jobs for you" list with four chips. The radius buttons sit inside "Near you".

New worker, one job picked. The hero holds level, jobs on the go and search; "Needs you now" shows the job to start; one jobs list with chips.

Chip "Near you" picked: the radius buttons (3, 10, 25, 50 km) appear; "Today" carries the Urgent count. "See all 142 jobs" opens the Find work list.

2c. The same header on every tab

Row 1 is identical everywhere: wordmark, Hire/Earn switch, notifications, messages, your face. Row 2 (every tab but Home) is the tab's name, one live line (counts that change) and at most one action on the right. Today Explore has no header, My tasks starts with a green summary card, My work with four stat tiles, Wallet with its balance card; each tab looks like a different app.

2d. My tasks and My work cards

One card, read top to bottom in two seconds: status (colour and words), what (title, nearby or remote), who and how much (face, name, rating, price and whether it is held), when, then one button. The button is filled only when the next step is yours; when you are waiting, it is an outline. Everything else (View details, Create similar, Top up, Close task) stays in the ⋯ menu as today.

Same blocks, wider: the ask box with its picture, what needs you as a short table, Only on TaskDrop and Ideas. The right rail keeps the live things together: active now, Just posted, Busy on TaskDrop, the helpers map.

Alive, without noise

What makes it feel active

  • The area row always carries a live number: "12 helpers active now", "23 open near you" (from the pulse the app already reads).
  • Just posted keeps moving as one slim line.
  • Faces have the green "active now" dot (presence is automatic, no go-live switch).
  • New offers show a small coral "1 new" badge on the card and on the tab.
  • Counts in the tab headers are real numbers, a dash while loading, never a fake 0.

Motion (all off with reduce motion)

  • Live dot: a soft ring every 2.2 s.
  • Ticker: about 40 px a second, still when reduced.
  • "1 new" badge: pops once (scale 0.7 to 1, 500 ms) when an offer lands.
  • Cards: the existing stagger (60 ms each, up to 6) on first load only.
  • Chip switch in Ideas and Jobs for you: cards cross-fade 250 ms (motion.ui).
  • No moving hero on Home (design card rule 8).

What gets quieter

  • One hero per screen, not three tinted cards in a row.
  • No helper text under every heading; the chips and cards say it.
  • No second copy of the same jobs or the same ideas.
  • Section gaps stay 32 px (D10) but there are half as many sections.
