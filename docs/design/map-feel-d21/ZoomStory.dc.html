MOUSE WHEELZooms round the mouse pointer, eased (about 250 ms a notch). The page itself never zooms.
TRACKPAD AND TOUCH PINCHThe map follows your fingers exactly, at any in-between zoom, then glides to rest.
DOUBLE-CLICK OR DOUBLE-TAPOne step closer, round that spot, in 300 ms. Drag flicks keep moving and slow down.
REDUCED MOTIONWith "reduce motion" on, zoom jumps without gliding and pins swap without moving.
