Why it feels like too many: #1, #3 and #4 explain the same thing three times, all in drawings, and a new person meets two of them before doing anything. #5 to #8 are not walkthroughs (one feature, safety, testers, app download), so they stay.

The one walkthrough

  • One thing, called "How TaskDrop works": 5 swipe cards, each a real app screen with one line under it. About 30 seconds.
  • Per role: Hire in jade, Earn in royal blue, a Hire / Earn switch at the top. Opens on the side the person is on.
  • Skip on every card. "Watch the 60-second video" on the first and last card, opens YouTube.
  • When: once, the first time the app opens (where the welcome story is now). Never again by itself.
  • Replay: Profile → "How TaskDrop works", Help & support, and the Home pill.
  • Gone: the drawn welcome story, its second copy on sign-up, the 13-tap tour and its offer after sign-up, the quick-look drawings.
