TaskDrop
"If you can ask for it, someone can drop it."
A unified reverse-marketplace: a Poster publishes an open-ended request with a benchmark price,
Workers pitch and negotiate quotes, and funds sit in escrow until the work is verified. An Android app for
the whole flow, plus a synced web app to view and monitor everything — with iOS to follow after success.

01 The concept
Existing platforms force people into narrow buckets — food delivery, home repair, digital freelancing —
with fixed pricing and no way to post an open-ended need. TaskDrop inverts the model: you post the
request, the market comes to you, and price is set by conversation instead of an algorithm. Once a
quote is locked, the money is handled the proven Fiverr way — full escrow up front, deliver, review, then
payout.
Dual-mode app
A seamless toggle between Post a Request (User mode) and Find Work (Worker mode) in one
account.
Dynamic quoting
Posters set a benchmark price; workers pitch quotes and negotiate directly, in-app. The poster picks the
quote he likes to lock the price.
Interactive reels
A Reels-style feed where workers showcase real skills and posters showcase the work to be done
in a video.
Interactive feed
A poster can post his task flagged as "Urgent" or "Unique" so it stands out to the right workers.

03 Who uses it
Poster / User mode
Posts requests, reviews bids, locks the worker quote he likes, pays the full amount into escrow, reviews
the delivery, and accepts or requests revisions.
Worker / Worker mode
Browses the feed and tasks, sends quotes, does the work, uploads proof, gets paid after the 7-day
clearing period.
Same account, both modes — separate reviews
One profile toggles between posting and working. Crucially, each account carries two separate rating/
review sets — one for its Poster role and one for its Worker role. They are shown separately by
role and are never merged into a single combined score.
Admin / ops ADMIN-ONLY · RBAC
Moderation, Resolution Center disputes, payout oversight, and cancellation monitoring. All admin pages
are visible only to the Admin role — enforced in UI and backend (row-level security). Posters and
Workers never see them.
04 The task lifecycle
Every task moves through one main line of states. Price is discovered by TaskDrop's quote-and-lock flow;
once the quote is locked, the money is handled the Fiverr way — escrow up front, deliver, review, payout.
OPEN 	01 · Request posted
Poster publishes the request — with no image, a single image, or a
video under 1 minute talking about the work. The poster must set a
time limit for the task in the quote itself, and can edit the time limit or
quote price anytime before lock. Workers can counter-quote with a
different price and/or a different time limit.
↓ Poster picks the worker quote he likes 	poster · lock

LOCKED 	02 · Price fixed, funds escrowed
The chosen quote becomes the fixed order amount and the poster pays the
full amount into escrow up front (contacts stay masked). Other bids stay
open — the poster may assign the task to multiple counter-quoting
workers at once.
SHOWN TO THE POSTER AT PAYMENT
"Don't worry — your amount won't be credited to the worker unless you're
satisfied. You hold the right to push the task to 'Completed'; until then your
money stays safe in escrow, and it will be credited back to you if the work
is not done."
↓ First worker to slide swipe-to-start wins 	worker · start ▸
TASK_STARTED 	03 · Work is underway · contacts revealed
The first worker to move the task to "Task Started" gets to proceed; the other
assigned workers can no longer proceed, and their escrow is auto-refunded to
the poster. Only now do both parties see each other's real contact details and
chat in-app. Payout mode is the poster's choice: milestones or one-time — after
starting, the worker can also request the poster to switch to milestones.
Default is one-time after completion.
↓ Worker uploads proof & marks done 	worker · work done
WORK_DONE 	04 · Delivery under review
Proof (photos / video / files) is submitted. No payout yet. The poster gets a 3-
day review window to Accept or Request Revisions.
↓ Poster taps Accept (or 3 days elapse) 	poster · accept
COMPLETED 	05 · Order accepted → clearing
Escrow is released to the worker minus the 20% commission and enters a flat
7-day clearing period. Both sides leave ratings and reviews, which stay public
on their profiles (by role).
Delivery & exception paths
WORK_DONE → REVISION_REQUESTED
Within the 3-day review window the poster can send the order back with notes. The worker redelivers; the
review window resets. No payout moves until the poster accepts or the window auto-completes.
WORK_DONE → AUTO_COMPLETED
If the poster neither accepts nor requests revisions within 3 days, the order auto-completes and escrow is
released to the worker — the Fiverr auto-accept safety net.

-- 5 of 17 --

When a poster assigns the task to several counter-quoting workers, the first worker to reach "Task
Started" locks the task; the remaining assignments are released, those workers can no longer proceed,
and any escrow paid for them is auto-refunded to the poster.
LOCKED → CANCELLED (before Task Started)
No grace window. The poster can cancel for free — no fee is charged to the worker in any case. The
only consequence is that the poster can leave a bad rating / review for the worker, since the worker did
not move the task to "Task Started" even after being assigned.
TASK_STARTED → CANCELLED (poster-initiated, after start)
If the poster cancels after the worker has started, the poster pays a 5% fine on the locked order value
and the worker can leave a bad rating / review for the poster. Logged in the Admin Cancellation
Monitor as cancelled_by = poster.
TASK_STARTED → CANCELLED (worker-initiated, after start)
The worker can also cancel after Task Started. Escrow is returned to the poster (Resolution Center
settles any partial work), the poster may leave a bad review for the worker, and this cancellation is
tracked in the Admin Cancellation Monitor as cancelled_by = worker.
TASK_STARTED → OVERDUE → CANCELLED (past quoted time, full refund)
When now > task_time_limit and the task isn't yet Work Done, it is flagged OVERDUE. The poster
can cancel with a FULL escrow refund and NO 5% fine (the fine applies only when cancelling a worker
still within time). The worker may get a bad review, and it is logged in the Admin Cancellation Monitor as
cancelled_by = poster, reason = overdue — so admins can track workers who repeatedly miss their quoted
time.
Any state → DISPUTED (Resolution Center)
Either side can open a dispute over a late, undone, or unsatisfactory delivery. Admin reviews evidence and
decides a full or partial refund; the outcome affects the worker's rating and standing.
05 Payments, solved the Fiverr way
1 · Price discovery stays TaskDrop's 	TaskDrop: post → quote → lock
The poster posts a benchmark with a required task time limit (both editable anytime); workers pitch
competing quotes, optionally countering on price and/or time. The poster picks the quote he likes, which
becomes the fixed order amount.
2 · Full payment up front into escrow 	Fiverr: pay in full at order
At lock, the poster pays the entire locked amount into escrow (plus a 3% poster service fee) before the
worker starts.

"Don't worry — your amount won't be credited to the worker unless you're satisfied. You hold the right to push
the task to 'Completed'; until then your money stays safe in escrow, and it will be credited back to you if the
work is not done."
3 · Deliver → review → accept / revise 	Fiverr: 3-day accept window
After Work Done, the poster has a 3-day review window to Accept or Request Revisions; unanswered orders
auto-complete and release escrow.
4 · Commission & poster fee 	Fiverr: 20% seller + buyer fee
TaskDrop takes a flat 20% worker commission on every completed order and a 3% poster service fee on
the locked value.
5 · Clearing period before withdrawal 	Fiverr: cleared-funds hold
Worker earnings clear a flat 7 days after completion for all workers, then move to a withdrawable balance.
6 · Cancellation rules 	No grace window
Before start: free, no worker fee; poster may leave a bad review. After start (poster): 5% fine + bad review,
logged for admin. After start (worker): escrow back to poster, bad review, logged for admin. Overdue (past
quoted time): poster cancels with full refund, no fine; bad review; logged as reason = overdue.
7 · Payout mode — poster's choice 	Milestones or one-time
Poster-set milestones, or worker-requested milestones after Task Started; otherwise a single one-time
payment after completion (default).

12 Admin cancellation monitor ADMIN-ONLY
A dedicated admin-only page tracking all cancellations after "Task Started" across three categories:
poster-initiated (5% fine), worker-initiated (worker abandons after starting), and overdue (poster
cancels a worker who ran past the quoted time — full refund, no fine).
What it shows
Split counts for poster-cancelled, worker-cancelled, and overdue cancellations, with time-period
filters (day / week / month).
Table: task, poster, worker, date, locked amount, cancelled_by, reason (overdue / normal), penalty/
refund, review left.
Repeat-offender views for posters, workers, and — importantly — workers who frequently go
overdue.
Backed by a cancellation log filtering TASK_STARTED → CANCELLED, keyed on cancelled_by
and reason.
