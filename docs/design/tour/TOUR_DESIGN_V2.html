The one-minute tour

It starts by itself right after onboarding. In five quick stops the customer does each thing instead of reading about it: they flip Hire and Earn, post a pretend task, pick an offer, watch the money get held and approve it. It takes about 60 seconds, and Skip is always one tap away.

What changed from the first design

Hands-on, not slides

The first design had nine read-only cards. This one has five stops, and each waits for the customer to tap the real thing that is lit up. They learn by doing it once.

Starts after onboarding

“Go to TaskDrop” lands on Home with the “Welcome to TaskDrop” toast. About a second later the tour sheet rises. One tap starts it, and Skip ends it for good.

A pretend task, start to finish

A practice task marked PRACTICE runs the whole loop: post, offers, money held, approve, paid. Nothing is saved and no money moves.

Follows their onboarding answer

Onboarding already asks “Get things done”, “Earn money” or “Both”. Hirers post and pay. Earners find a job, get picked and get paid. “Both” sees the hiring side first and can watch the earning side in 30 seconds.

4. What we took from apps that do this well

Do it, don't read it (Duolingo)

Duolingo puts you in a real lesson within the first minute instead of explaining lessons. Our version is the practice task: the customer posts, picks and approves once, so the money flow is something they have done, not just read.

Main job first (Slack)

Slack's first steps get you sending a message, its main job, by doing it. Our first real stop after the switch is posting a request, TaskDrop's main job.

A sandbox that is clearly fake (Notion)

Notion starts you on a sample page you can play with. Our practice task carries PRACTICE and the offers carry EXAMPLE (the tag the app already uses on Compare), so nobody thinks a real job was posted.

One ring, one line, one tap

Apple's and Android's design guides both say to teach at the moment of use. Each stop lights up one control, says one or two short lines and asks for one tap. Progress dots and Skip are always on screen, and there are never more than five stops.

6. Rules it keeps

Pretend means pretend

  • The practice post, offers, chat and payment are drawn by the tour itself. Nothing is sent to the server, nothing is posted and no money moves.
  • The only real thing the customer changes is the Hire/Earn switch. The tour puts it back the way it was at the end, and the customer ends on the tab they started on.
  • Fee, approval window and clearing time are read live from packages/rules. The worker commission is admin-set and is not quoted; the Earn side points to “How fees work”.

Always in their control

  • Skip is at the top of every card. The phone's Back button and Esc on the web also skip.
  • Replay any time: tap your photo, then Take the app tour in the account menu, next to Help & support.
  • On the staging app the tour waits until the “How staging works” card is closed, so the two never stack.

Web, Android and later iOS

  • One shared component and one list of steps for all platforms. All words live in one strings file.
  • Desktop web: the same stops, with the ring on the side menu's items (the switch, Post a request, Messages and so on). Sheets open as a centred dialog.
  • Android draws the five tabs itself, so on stop 5 the tour draws its own frame over the bar instead of circling a single tab. This gets checked on your phone during the build.

Everyone can use it

  • Each card is announced as “Tour step n of 5” with its title. The lit-up control keeps its normal label.
  • Screen reader and keyboard users also get a Next button, because they cannot see the ring. On the web, Enter does the lit-up action.
  • Light, dark and System themes; colours come only from theme.ts; buttons and targets are at least 44 px.

7. What I need from you

1. A change to the plan's “read-only” rule

The plan says the tour never flips the real switch. You asked for people to click and feel it, so in this design they flip the real Hire/Earn switch (put back at the end) and play with a pretend task inside the tour. Nothing is saved. OK?

2. Where “tour seen” is remembered

Per device (my suggestion, no database change): someone on a new phone is offered the tour once more. Per account: seen once, never offered again on any device; it needs one additive migration.

3. The “Both” customers

People who picked “Both” do the hiring side, and the last card offers See the earning side (30 s). Keep it, or show them the hiring side only?
