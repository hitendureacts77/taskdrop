4. Controls and states

On every card

  • Step n of 9 at top left; Skip top right (Close on the last card).
  • Progress dots: nine; the current one is a wider jade pill, finished ones are a soft jade, the rest are grey.
  • Back (outlined) and Next (filled). Step 1 has no Back. Step 9 swaps them for Post a request, Find work and Back.
  • Buttons are 44 px tall with 44 px touch targets, pill shaped, as the app’s other primary buttons.

Look

  • Dimmed screen at about 62% dark, a jade ring (blue when on Earn) on the thing being pointed at, and one soft outer ring from the brand ripple.
  • Card: surface colour, 22 px corners, title 18/800 Plus Jakarta Sans, body 14/400. Light has a soft shadow; dark has a 1 px border only.
  • Colours come only from theme.ts tokens. Jade = hiring and protected money, blue = earning, gold = the EXAMPLE tag. White text only on deep shades.
  • Light, dark and System follow the app’s theme.

Keyboard and screen reader

  • Each card is a dialog announcing “Tour step n of 9” and its title; focus moves to the card and returns to where it was when the tour ends.
  • Web: Tab cycles inside the card; Enter or Right arrow = Next, Left arrow = Back, Esc = Skip.
  • The dimmed area is hidden from the screen reader; the pointed-at control is announced by its normal label.

Rules it keeps

  • Read-only: no money moves, no data changes, no signed-in action is opened. The Hire/Earn switch in step 2 is a picture; the real switch is not flipped.
  • The app is left as it was found (same tab, same mode).
  • All words live in one strings file; numbers (3%, 3 days, 7 days) are read live, so changing a rule never leaves the tour stale.
  • The worker commission is not quoted; “How fees work” (step 8) is where it is shown. No legal or compliance claims appear in the tour.

  • Opening: the screen dims over 250 ms, then the card rises 24 px and fades in over 360 ms.
  • Between steps: the ring slides and resizes to the next target over 360 ms while the words cross-fade over 250 ms; going Back runs the same in reverse. Cards that sit above the tab bar slide sideways with the arrow, they do not jump.
  • Step 1: three brand ripples spread from the drop once. Step 4: the drop button bobs gently as it already does on Home (1.6 s). Step 9: the check pops in with the app’s spring and two ripples, 1.2 s, no confetti.
  • Step 2: the Hire and Earn pills trade colour (jade to blue) over 250 ms. The real switch is untouched.
  • Closing: dim and card fade out over 220 ms.
  • Reduced motion (the device setting, read the same way the app does today): no sliding, scaling, ripples, bobbing or stagger. The ring jumps to the next target and the card text swaps with a 120 ms fade at most. Every step stays fully readable and usable, and nothing is explained by motion alone.

Where the plan draft and the live app differ (I followed the app)

  • “Post a task” vs the app’s “Post a request”. Every live button says “Post a request” (side menu, drop button, Home). The final card uses the app’s word. If you want “task”, the live buttons would have to change too, so I have not done it quietly.
  • “Explore becomes Find work” is only true on the Android/iOS tab bar. The web bar and the desktop menu keep the word “Explore” on both sides. Step 2 therefore only promises “My Tasks becomes My Work”, which is true everywhere. Changing the web labels would alter an existing screen, so that is your call, not part of the tour.
  • The account menu also has “My complaints”, which the plan’s list leaves out. The step 8 copy names only the rows people look for first.
  • The AI writer. The post screen’s own notes say the writing is done on the device with no AI, so the tour does not mention an AI writer.
  • The post button lives on Home only on phones, so the tour runs on Home and puts the person back where they were.

Decision 1. Does the tour start by itself once, or only when someone picks it?

Offer it once after onboarding, with a clear SkipMY SUGGESTION

Only when someone picks it
Nothing is ever shown unasked. Entry B is dropped; the menu row is the only door. Quieter, but fewer people will ever see it.

Decision 2. Where does the app remember that the tour was seen?

Per devicePLAN DEFAULT
No database change. Someone on a new phone, a cleared browser or a reinstall is offered it once more. Simple and safe.
Per account
Seen once, never offered again on any device. Needs one additive database migration (the old app is untouched, ground rule 4), so it needs your go-ahead to apply on the test project.

If you do not say, I will use per device. Replaying from the account menu always works either way.
